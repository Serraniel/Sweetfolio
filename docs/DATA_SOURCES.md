# Data sources of the crisis tester

The crisis tester (requirements CR-1 … CR-7) extends asset histories backwards with proxy series.

## Bundled with the app

`static/data/crisis-rates.json` is generated by `node scripts/crisis-data.mjs` from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis
(<https://fred.stlouisfed.org/>). The series are US government data in the public domain; FRED asks for a citation:

- `DTB3` — 3-Month Treasury Bill Secondary Market Rate (Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, H.15)
- `DGS1`, `DGS2`, `DGS10`, `DGS20`, `DGS30` — Market Yield on U.S. Treasury Securities at Constant Maturity (H.15)
- `EXGEUS` — German Deutsche Mark to U.S. Dollar Spot Exchange Rate (H.10, monthly)
- `DEXUSEU` — U.S. Dollar to Euro Spot Exchange Rate (H.10)

Bond total returns are modelled from these yields as constant-maturity par bonds (carry, duration and convexity; Swinkels 2019,
"Treasury bond return data starting in 1962"). Before 1971 the USD/DEM Bretton Woods parities are used.

## Downloaded by the user

Equity and gold index histories (S&P 500, MSCI World, Nasdaq-100, MSCI Emerging Markets, STOXX Europe 600, DAX, gold spot) are
fetched from Onvista in the user's browser when they choose "Download crisis history". They are stored only in that browser,
are not part of backups and are not distributed with Sweetfolio.

## Not used

- LBMA gold prices (removed from FRED in 2022 for licensing reasons).
- Kenneth R. French Data Library and World Bank commodity prices: their redistribution terms are not confirmed, so nothing is
  bundled. As a consequence gold has no proxy before 1995.
